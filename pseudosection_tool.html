Groundwater screening from known wells ใช้บ่อที่รู้ผลแล้วช่วยประเมินจุดอื่น

Tell the tool where water was (or was not) found at some soundings. It learns the resistivity range of the water-bearing zone in your area, then rates every other sounding, suggests a depth, warns about points that look like dry wells, and can draw the water-bearing zone on the cross-section. More wells = better (use at least 3 with water).
